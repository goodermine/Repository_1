FAQ

      

        Is my data uploaded?
        
No. The QR code is generated entirely on your device with the open-source node-qrcode library — nothing is sent to any server. (Many online QR tools quietly send your link to their servers; this one doesn't.)

      

      

        What's a "Contact" QR?
        
It encodes a vCard — when someone scans it, their phone offers to save your name, phone, email, organization, and website straight into Contacts.

      

      

        Where can I use the QR code?
        
Anywhere — download the PNG and put it on a poster, business card, slide, or website. There's no tracking and the code never expires (it points directly at your link/text, not through a redirect).
